Lux AEterna
Lux AEterna is a fast-paced 2D top-down action-survival game built in Unity for the Nimble Game Jam. Stationed at the center of a fading world, you command a radiant power core emitting a high-energy light beam—your sole line of defense against encroaching shadow entities. Survival requires precise beam rotation and careful battery management, as firing drains power rapidly and risks locking down your systems from overheating. In a unique twist on core defense, defeated foes aren't just destroyed; bathing their remains in your light purifies them into automated builder bots that actively repair your core's integrity while you hold back the swarm.
C# · Unity

A project made during 48-hours Junction x Aalto Defence Hackathon. An automated regional intelligence engine designed for autonomous drone operations.
